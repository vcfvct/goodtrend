# Misc

## MD Sales Quarterly Report required

[Maryland Tax Connect](https://mdtaxconnect.gov/rptp/portal/home)

Maryland Department of Assessments and Taxation requires all business entities to file a Sales and Use Tax Return each quarter, even if no tax is due. The report is due on the 20th day of the month following the end of each quarter.

## MD business Annual Report

The easiest way to fill out and file your annual report is through SDAT’s [Maryland Business Express](https://egov.maryland.gov/BusinessExpress/Account/LogOn) online platform.

You can submit an MD annual report on or before April 15 of each year.
